# Genetics and evolution (random facts)

## DNA → RNA → protein
- **Transcription:** RNA polymerase builds RNA from a DNA template strand (5'→3').
- **Translation:** Ribosomes read mRNA codons; tRNAs bring amino acids.
- **Codon:** 3-nucleotide mRNA sequence coding for an amino acid or stop.
  - **Start codon:** AUG (codes methionine in many organisms).
  - **Stop codons:** UAA, UAG, UGA.

## Mutations (simple categories)
- **Point mutation:** Single nucleotide change.
  - **Silent:** No amino acid change.
  - **Missense:** Different amino acid.
  - **Nonsense:** Creates a stop codon → truncated protein.
- **Insertion/deletion (indel):** Can cause a **frameshift** if not in multiples of 3.

## Mendelian vs non-Mendelian snapshots
- **Mendel’s laws:** Segregation and independent assortment (with caveats like linkage).
- **Incomplete dominance:** Heterozygote phenotype is intermediate.
- **Codominance:** Both alleles expressed (classic example often taught: AB blood type).

## Evolution quickies
- **Natural selection** requires:
  1) Variation
  2) Heritability
  3) Differential reproductive success
- **Genetic drift:** Random fluctuations in allele frequency, strongest in small populations.
- **Founder effect:** Drift when a small group starts a new population.
- **Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium** assumptions (idealized): no selection, mutation, migration; random mating; infinitely large population.
