Microbiology (simple notes)

Horizontal gene transfer (HGT) = genes moving between organisms (not parent to child).
Common methods:
- Transformation: uptake of free DNA from the environment
- Transduction: DNA moved by viruses that infect bacteria (bacteriophages)
- Conjugation: DNA passed through direct cell-to-cell contact (often via a plasmid)

Biofilms:
- Communities of microbes stuck to a surface, embedded in a protective matrix
- Biofilms can make bacteria harder to kill (reduced antibiotic penetration, slower growth, protection)
