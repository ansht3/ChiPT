Plant transport and photosynthesis (simple notes)

Xylem:
- Moves water + minerals from roots to leaves
- Driven mostly by transpiration (water evaporating from leaves pulls water upward)

Phloem:
- Moves sugars (like sucrose) from sources to sinks
- Sources: leaves (photosynthesis)
- Sinks: roots, fruits, growing tissues

Stomata:
- Tiny pores on leaves that control gas exchange and water loss
- Open for CO2 intake, but that increases water loss
