# Ecology and physiology (mixed notes)

## Energy flow and trophic levels
- **Primary producers** (plants, algae, some bacteria) capture energy (often via photosynthesis).
- **Trophic levels:** Producers → primary consumers → secondary consumers → ...
- **Energy transfer is inefficient:** A common rule-of-thumb taught is ~10% transfer between trophic levels (varies widely in real ecosystems).

## Nutrient cycles
- **Carbon cycle:** CO₂ fixed by photosynthesis, returned by respiration, decomposition, combustion.
- **Nitrogen cycle:**
  - **Nitrogen fixation:** N₂ → ammonia (NH₃/NH₄⁺) by certain microbes.
  - **Nitrification:** NH₄⁺ → NO₂⁻ → NO₃⁻.
  - **Denitrification:** NO₃⁻ → N₂ (returns nitrogen to atmosphere).

## Homeostasis and feedback
- **Negative feedback:** Stabilizes a variable (e.g., body temperature regulation).
- **Positive feedback:** Amplifies change (e.g., some hormone cascades, blood clotting initiation).

## Respiration vs photosynthesis (high level)
- **Cellular respiration:** Extracts energy from organic molecules to produce ATP (often using O₂ as final electron acceptor in aerobic respiration).
- **Photosynthesis:** Uses light energy to build sugars (in oxygenic photosynthesis, water is split and O₂ released).
